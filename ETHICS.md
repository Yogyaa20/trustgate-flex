# Ethics Statement

## Core Principle

TrustGate Flex is built to protect organisational intellectual property — not to surveil, score, or discipline individual employees. Every design decision reflects this distinction.

## Fairness

**Time-of-day has zero weight in the risk engine.**

A flexible worker who regularly works at night is treated identically to a day worker with the same device trust, project assignment, and download rate. The system is designed to avoid penalising:

- Remote workers
- Night-shift workers
- Workers in unusual time zones
- Workers with approved irregular schedules

We block on volume and device anomaly, not on schedule. This is not an afterthought — it is a formal design constraint coded into the risk engine from day one.

## No Surveillance

TrustGate Flex does NOT:
- Log keystrokes, mouse movements, or screen content
- Record audio or video
- Monitor communications or email
- Track physical location via GPS
- Build psychological profiles
- Generate employee performance scores

## Human Override

Every block or restriction can be overridden by a human manager. False-positive appeals are supported. No automated decision is permanent at the BLOCK level without human review available.

## Transparency

Every risk score shows a per-factor breakdown. Every decision shows which policy rule fired and why. Any administrator can read the formula, verify the logic, and explain any decision to HR, legal, or management in plain English.

## Consent and Disclosure

In any production deployment, all monitored users would be informed in advance of:
- What context data is collected
- How access decisions are made
- How to appeal a decision they believe is incorrect
- Who reviews appeals

## Scope

This prototype uses only synthetic data. No real employee data is collected, stored, or processed.

## Intended Use

TrustGate Flex is intended to protect organisational IP from bulk exfiltration by insiders — not to surveil employees, create productivity metrics, or support disciplinary action beyond access revocation.
